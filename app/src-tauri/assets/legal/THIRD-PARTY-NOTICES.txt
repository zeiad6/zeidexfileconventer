LibreOffice (MPL-2.0), Calibre (GPL-3.0), whisper.cpp (MIT), ExifTool (Artistic-2.0),
ZBar (LGPL-2.1), Real-ESRGAN (BSD-3-Clause), rembg (MIT); on Windows and macOS also

The Rust and JavaScript dependencies keep their own licences; `cargo deny`/`cargo
about` and `npm ls` report them for the exact versions in Cargo.lock and
package-lock.json.
